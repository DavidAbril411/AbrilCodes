About Me

I'm David Abril Perrig, a software engineer and the founder of Abril Codes, a boutique studio based in Córdoba, Argentina. I design the architecture and write the core of every project: you don't talk to a salesperson, you talk to the engineer building the system.

Three years shipping backends, native mobile apps and self-managed infrastructure for startups and clients across the US and Europe: a geolocated marketplace, a logistics CRM with a C++20 routing engine, point-of-sale systems and autonomous AI agents.

Final-year Software Engineering student at UBP with an 8.75/10 GPA, and a certified electronics technician: I understand the software and the hardware it runs on.

How I work with AI

I use AI as a precision tool, not as a careless code generator. Every architectural decision, every data contract and every system boundary is mine. When AI ships inside a product, it ships with guardrails: in Nightdesk I built a verifier that matches every quote against the source document character by character, and rejects the draft when it doesn't exist.
